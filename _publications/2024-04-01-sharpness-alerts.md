In-Context Sharpness as Alerts: An Inner Representation Perspective for Hallucination Mitigation
========================================================================================

This paper proposes using in-context sharpness as alerts to mitigate hallucinations in large language models from an inner representation perspective. The method detects potential hallucinations by analyzing the sharpness of model representations in the context window.

### Key Contributions

- Proposed a novel hallucination detection mechanism using in-context sharpness
- Provided an inner representation perspective on hallucination mitigation
- Evaluated the approach on multiple benchmark datasets

### Authors

Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Junteng Liu, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He
