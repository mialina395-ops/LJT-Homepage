SynLogic: Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond
================================================================================================

This paper presents SynLogic, a novel approach for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale to improve logical reasoning capabilities in large language models. The method addresses the challenge of generating high-quality reasoning datasets that can be verified for correctness.

### Key Contributions

- Developed a scalable method for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data
- Improved logical reasoning capabilities in LLMs through structured data synthesis
- Evaluated the method on multiple reasoning benchmarks
