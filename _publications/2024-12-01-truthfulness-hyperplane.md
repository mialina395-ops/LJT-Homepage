On the Universal Truthfulness Hyperplane Inside LLMs
========================================================================================

This paper explores the concept of a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models. We investigate the underlying mechanisms that determine the truthfulness of LLM outputs and identify a hyperplane that separates truthful from untruthful responses.

### Key Contributions

- Discovered a universal truthfulness hyperplane in LLMs
- Provided theoretical analysis of truthfulness in language models
- Evaluated the findings on multiple LLM architectures
