On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding
========================================================================================

This paper investigates the perception bottleneck in Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for chart understanding. We analyze the limitations of current VLMs in interpreting visual data presented in chart format and propose methods to address these challenges.

### Key Contributions

- Identified the perception bottleneck in VLMs for chart understanding
- Proposed methods to improve VLM performance on chart-based tasks
- Evaluated the approach on multiple chart datasets
