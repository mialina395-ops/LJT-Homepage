C-Eval: A Multi-Level Multi-Discipline Chinese Evaluation Suite for Foundation Models
========================================================================================

This paper presents C-Eval, a comprehensive evaluation suite for Chinese foundation models across multiple disciplines and evaluation levels. The suite addresses the lack of comprehensive evaluation benchmarks for Chinese language models.

### Key Contributions

- Created a multi-level, multi-discipline evaluation suite for Chinese foundation models
- Covered a wide range of tasks and domains in Chinese NLP
- Provided baseline results for multiple Chinese models
